PART 01 · FILM ① CASH COW & STABLE BASE

Mưa Rào

The Rain ShowerNOSTALGIC YOUTH · FAMILY ROMANCE · 100 MIN · NOV 2027

"Ten summers may pass — we are still under the same rainbow."

"Mười mùa hè trôi qua, ta vẫn dưới chung một cầu vồng."

INSPIRED BY HWANG SUN-WON'S "SONAGI" · SCREENPLAY ISAAC WEB

WHY THIS FILM

Everyone has a summer they never forget.
When it rains, we stop.

Hwang Sun-won's "Sonagi", a story every Korean knows, is reborn in the red dragon-fruit fields of Bình Thuận, on the Cà Ty riverbank, on the bare feet of Vietnamese children. Korea's jeong (情) and Vietnam's tình cảm are remarkably alike.

Proven story × local feeling

The structure of a Korean national short story that has survived 60 years, fully reinterpreted through rural Bình Thuận in 1995 and Ho Chi Minh City in 2005.

Retro mise-en-scène

Yahoo Messenger, the Nokia 1100, Tiger Cup football fever. Not just viewing but 'an experience of memory'.

Cry while smiling

No melodramatic excess. Butterflies for teens and twenties, childhood nostalgia for parents in their 30s–40s — a well-made family film for every generation.

"The rain shower stops quickly. But the memory of it is forever." — the film's last line, and the whole of this project.

SYNOPSIS

The summer of 1995 and the rain of 2005 — two times, two colours.

PART 1 · 1995 HÀM MỸ VILLAGE, BÌNH THUẬNWarm, saturated gold

Phú (10), a pale city boy sent to the countryside to recover from a heart condition, and Ánh (9), a fearless, big-hearted barefoot village girl, share the most radiant summer of their lives among red dragon-fruit fields and the Cà Ty river. After saving Ánh in a great typhoon, Phú collapses and is flown to America for emergency surgery — a farewell of pinky fingers pressed to the airport glass, promising 'forever'.

PART 2 · 2005 HO CHI MINH CITYCool, urban blue

Ánh (19), now a university student still keeping unanswered letters, goes on a blind date in a friend's place. There she meets Phú (20), who lost the village address to post-surgery amnesia and found his memory again through a rainbow on a rainy day — a tearful reunion. A film that ends not in sorrow but in comfort.

4 SIGNATURE SEQUENCES① First meeting in the dragon-fruit field② Rain shower on the Cà Ty & the hen 'Bạch Chỉ'③ Typhoon climax & airport-glass farewell④ Saigon café 2005, reunion under the rainbow

FOUR MOTIFS

Layered into the audience's subconscious: rain · dragon fruit · rainbow · boat

Rain Mưa

Life-giving rain, a brief but intense encounter. The 'rain shower scene', shot at 48fps, is the heart of the film.

Dragon fruit Thanh long

Thorny outside, white and soft inside — a metaphor for Phú himself and for true friendship.

Rainbow Cầu vồng

What remains after parting, the symbol of a promise. First seen in the rain scene, last seen over the river at sunset.

The boat 'Mãi Mãi' Con thuyền

The paper boat 'Forever' that Phú floats down the river. The audience remembers it with sadness — until the ending flips its meaning and makes them smile again.

Music: the clear line of the Vietnamese đàn tranh + piano + a distant children's choir — the film's sound signature. Images are AI concept stills, unrelated to actual casting or photography.

Kỳ Tích

The MiracleHUMAN SPORTS EPIC INSPIRED BY TRUE EVENTS · ~100 MIN · 2028 OLYMPIC SEASON

"Well done." — the one word a master saved for a lifetime, crossing borders to become history.

"Giỏi lắm." — Một lời khen cả đời chắt chiu, vượt biên giới để trở thành lịch sử.

ISAAC STUDIO FOUNDING FILM · SCREENPLAY ISAAC WEB

WHY THIS FILM

Sydney, 28 September 2000. Vietnam won its first Olympic medal.
The medal was in taekwondo — and the coach was Korean.

The warmth of defeat over the roar of victory; a master–disciple bond across the wounds of borders and generations. Through the worn black belt of a father who fought in the Vietnam War, two generations of two nations that once met with guns reconcile in dobok and belt — a Vietnamese film telling a Vietnamese story.

Olympics

The weight of a nation's first medal. A 2028 Olympic-season release brings the fever outside the cinema inside.

Taekwondo

Where Korea's story as the birthplace of the art meets the sport of Vietnam's first medal. The Kukkiwon demo team is the marketing engine.

Human triumph

A floating-market girl erased by the federation and a coach cast out by the IMF crisis. Two erased people write history.

Friendship

Four layers: master and disciple, father and daughter, neighbours, a former pupil. One "Well done" outweighs a gold medal.

ISAAC STUDIO FOUNDING FILM

A work of fiction inspired by true events. Based on the adapted screenplay (110 scenes, locked) which avoids identification with real persons or organisations; factual review is handled by separate advisors.

SYNOPSIS

Ho Chi Minh City, 1997: two erased people. Sydney, 2000: a miracle.

ACT 1 · HO CHI MINH CITY, 1997

Kim Mu-yeol, a former national-team coach expelled from Korea's Taereung training centre by the IMF crisis, teaches a children's class in a run-down Saigon dojang. At the Mekong floating market he discovers Ánh, a girl who leaps from boat to boat carrying four bowls of noodle soup without spilling a drop — a genius of balance, erased from the federation by injury and poverty.

ACT 2 · TRAINING ON THE MEKONG

A gruff master who treats athletes only as numbers and records and has never once said a word of praise; a pupil who only ever says 'I'm fine' (Không sao) even when hurt. Under a tin-roof dojang and on rocking noodle boats, through a 'floating-market mock Olympics' where the neighbours serve as referees and sparring partners, Ánh reaches the Sydney 2000 stage.

ACT 3 · SYDNEY 2000

After four brutal bouts in one day, the final opponent is Seo Ji-won, the prize pupil Mu-yeol once raised in Korea. With every pattern read by the Korean analysts, Ánh throws away the game plan and counters to the rhythm of Mekong oars — and loses. With silver around her neck she cries for the first time, "Master… it hurts," and Mu-yeol finally says it aloud — "Well done."

SIGNATURE SEQUENCES

Four scenes that sell the film in a single trailer cut.

① The flying kick of the floating market

Ánh's astonishing opening stunt: leaping between rocking noodle boats without spilling a drop of broth.

② The floating-market mock Olympics

To rehearse Sydney's four-bouts-a-day schedule, the whole market turns referee and sparring partner from dawn to night — a joyous, warm ensemble.

③ The Sydney final and 'the rhythm of the Mekong'

With all her data read, in the moment of truth she abandons the game plan and counters to the beat of rowing — breathless taekwondo action.

④ The black-belt exchange on the podium & "Well done"

Gold and silver medallists tie each other's black belts — the etiquette of martial artists — and, after 25 years, a master's words finally come from deep within.

REVENUE WATERFALL & FIVE LAYERS OF INVESTOR PROTECTION

Designed so that investors' money comes back first.

1
Exhibitor & distribution fees

Exhibitor share and the distributor's base fee are deducted from gross box office.

2
P&A recoupment

P&A advanced by the distributor and marketing consortium (Mưa Rào 1X / Kỳ Tích 2X) is recouped first.

3
100% principal priority

From all net receipts — theatrical, OTT, international sales, placement — the investor group (our seed, distributor, external investors, pari passu pro rata) recovers the VND 60bn principal first.

Cross-collateralization: both films' settlements are pooled — if one underperforms, the other's surplus repays principal first.

4
Net profit split

After principal, all net profit is split 55–60% investor group : 40–45% producer/creatives.

5
Overrun control · escrow

All Star Production fixed-price turnkey contract + project escrow with milestone approvals + monthly spend reports for full transparency.

BOX OFFICE→EXHIBITOR · DISTRIBUTOR→P&A→100% PRINCIPAL (CROSS-COLLATERAL)→55–60% OF NET PROFIT TO INVESTORS
